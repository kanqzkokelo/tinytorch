## Hidden 896 Fix — Steps

- **Hypothesis:** `nb=28` small, `blockDim.y=8` for `M=896` gives `grid 28 blocks` low occupancy; LM head `M=151k` gets `4735 blocks` high occupancy. Hidden needs `blockDim.y=16 or 32` or `2-rows-per-warp` vs `4-rows` switch at `M<2048`.
- **Actions:**
  1. In `kernels/gemv_q4_cuda.cu` try `gemv_dims4` `y=16` for `M<=1024`, `y=8` for larger. Also try `k_gemv_q4_0_v2` for `M=896` vs `v4`.
  2. Ensure `__ldg` for `W` and `x` coalesced: `float4 x` load is `b*32` with `lane` stride 32 — ok for 151k but for 896 `b` loops 28 times with `lane=0..31` each do 28/32=0.875 blocks per lane — under-utilized. Try `lane` loop over `nb` not `nb/32`?
  3. Measure honest: `nvcc -O3 -arch=sm_86 -o /tmp/micro_v4 tools/micro_v4.cu && /tmp/micro_v4 896 896` 200 per-iter median; same `4864 896`; target `>=130 GB/s`.
  4. Engine dispatch: `kernels/qwen2_cuda.cu` `gemv_q4` picks V2 vs V4 by `M`; ensure `M=896` picks faster after tuning.
- **Acceptance:** Both shapes `>=130 GB/s` median, `max|V2-V4|=0`, `bench_llm --ctx 32` median `272 -> >=300` (track toward `330`).

## 131k FA Fix — Steps

- **Hypothesis:** `SPLIT=16` at `131k` gives `8192 tokens/split`? Actually `chunk = (131072+16-1)/16=8192`, `S=16` -> `16*14*32=7168 threads` per layer, each block loads `BC=64` tokens via `tid` strided `total_blocks=64*4=256` loads per tile, `256/224 threads` ~1 load per thread, then `bc_active=64` online softmax loop over 64 tokens serial per warp — low ILP.
- **Actions:**
  1. Try `SPLIT=32 or 64` at large `N_CTX>32768` to reduce per-split chunk `4096 or 2048`, more parallelism, less `p_m/p_l` combine cost (`S*14` floats). Measure `tools/micro_paged_fa2 131072` median p50 with `S=16/32/64`.
  2. Try `FA2_DECODE_BC=32 or 128` to change `sK_q` shmem `64*64=4096 B` -> `32*64=2048` or `128*64=8192` trade occupancy.
  3. Ensure `sm_86` shmem `100KB` not exceeded, keep `__syncthreads` after cooperative load.
  4. Optional: bypass paged at `ctx<=2048` -> use `k_flash_gqa` non-paged if faster short ctx (profile showed paged `0.060 ms @2048` L2-bound vs non-paged maybe faster).
- **Acceptance:** `micro_paged_fa2 131072` median `<=0.80 ms/layer` (3x), `ctx128 bench_llm` `180 -> >=240`, `ctx131k` 24-layer total `<=19 ms` (vs `60 ms` now), `gate_m6 7/7` still.
